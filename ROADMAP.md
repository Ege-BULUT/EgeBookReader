# Roadmap

## Done on Android

Reader, page turning, themes, typography, actual book mode, image filters, colorization, read aloud, picture viewer, all common formats, right-to-left reading, the webtoon strip, and a library with favourites, lists, categories, tags, sorting, filters and book details (0.1.7). Highlights and notes, panel-by-panel comics, reading statistics, ratings and series, imports from a folder, Calibre or Wi-Fi, a home-screen shelf and read aloud from the lock screen (0.2.0). Search inside a book, pages voiced ahead offline, natural Turkish voices and the reader's own voice, an audiobook player with listening shelves and audiobook files, comic spreads, AI upscaling and speech balloon translation, e-ink mode, automatic backups, migration, end-to-end and performance tests (0.5.0).

## Next

Everything stays local and serverless. There is no server of our own. The phone talks straight to public services (Open Library, Wikipedia), models download from GitHub releases, and the free Vercel site and its free add-ons are the only hosting.

1. **Interface languages.** English and Turkish today. Then Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French and Hindi.
2. **More platforms.** iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux from the same code.
